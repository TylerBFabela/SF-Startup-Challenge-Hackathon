# Scoring rubric (all roles)

Score each criterion from 1 to 5 using only evidence written in the candidate's file.

1. **Skills fit**: how well their stated skills and experience match the job's requirements.
2. **Shipped impact**: evidence they built and released real things (products, features, open-source
   projects, integrations) and what happened after release.
3. **Ownership**: took responsibility end to end, communicates clearly, learns fast.

Total is the sum (max 15). Advance at 11 or more, hold at 8 to 10, pass at 7 or less.
